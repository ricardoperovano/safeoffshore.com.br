Any work built on, over, under or along Brazilian jurisdictional waters needs a **favourable opinion (*parecer*) from the Brazilian Navy** before construction starts. That covers piers, quays, ramps, landfills, rock armour, dredging, moorings, submarine cables and pipelines. Applications go to the local Capitania dos Portos (Harbour Master's Office), or the Delegacia or Agência that covers the area. The rule is **NORMAM-303/DPC, the Maritime Authority Standards for Works and Related Activities in Waters under Brazilian Jurisdiction**. The Navy's opinion deals with safety of navigation and the use of the waterway. It does not replace environmental, federal property or municipal licences. This guide is for foreign EPC contractors, terminal developers and their Brazilian partners.

> This guide summarizes public rules as of October 2026. It is not legal advice. Check the current text of NORMAM-303/DPC and confirm documents, fees and timing with the local Capitania dos Portos before planning.

## Which version applies

NORMAM-303 has been revised several times, and documents from earlier projects often cite older numbers:

| Instrument | Effect |
|---|---|
| NORMAM-11/DPC | Former standard for works in Brazilian waters |
| Portaria DPC nº 103 of 30/08/2023 | Amended NORMAM-11 and renamed it NORMAM-303/DPC, in force from 02/10/2023 |
| Portaria DPC/DGN/MB nº 157 of 15/01/2025 | Replaced the 2023 text |
| Portaria DPC/DGN/MB nº 203 of 28/04/2026 | Approved the current NORMAM-303/DPC and revoked Portaria 157 |

If your specifications, environmental studies or local partner's checklists refer to NORMAM-11, check them against the 2026 text.

## Which works need an opinion

The gov.br service page covers public and private works located under, over or along Brazilian jurisdictional waters. Typical cases include:

- piers, jetties, quays, dolphins, floating pontoons and ramps;
- landfills, breakwaters, rock armour and coastal protection;
- capital and maintenance dredging, and spoil disposal;
- mooring buoys and single-point moorings, which NORMAM-601/DHN, the standard for aids to navigation, refers to NORMAM-303;
- submarine cables, pipelines and outfalls.

The 2026 text also has provisions on the refurbishment or maintenance of existing works, the regularization of works built without an opinion, and dredging and landfills. Nautical events such as regattas and races are handled by the local Capitania under its own port rules (*Normas e Procedimentos da Capitania*, NPCP). Lead times vary from one Capitania to another, so check the NPCP for the area.

Works that interfere with aids to navigation also need the Navy's consent under Law 6.421/1977. In practice, a new terminal often needs a NORMAM-303 opinion for the structures and a NORMAM-601 project for buoyage and lights.

## How the process works

According to the gov.br service page (updated 15/12/2025), the steps are:

1. **Read NORMAM-303** for the type of work.
2. **Optionally, ask for a preliminary meeting** with the Capitania, Delegacia or Agência to clarify the procedure. This is worth doing for large or unusual projects.
3. **Book an appointment**, online through the Navy's digital services platform (available at about 50 offices) or in person. The applicant needs a gov.br account.
4. **Pay the federal fee slip (GRU)**. Payment by PIX or credit card allows immediate booking. A bank slip can take up to three working days to clear.
5. **File the documents** and receive an access key to track the process. Requests for further information and the final document are notified by email or text message if contact details are registered.

**Fees and validity**, according to the gov.br service page:

| Item | Value or rule |
|---|---|
| Analysis of the process and issue of the opinion | R$ 181.00 |
| On-site inspection of the work location | R$ 242.00 |
| Validity of a favourable opinion | 4 years |
| Dredging | Validity set by the Capitania with jurisdiction |
| Renewal | Requested from the office that issued the opinion. Full documentation is not needed if the approved project has not changed |
| Analysis time | Not estimated |

Confirm the fees and rules on the day you file. The page does not give a standard analysis time, so build a margin into the schedule.

## Documents to prepare

The full list depends on the type of work and is set out in NORMAM-303. For Espírito Santo, the Capitania's guidance includes an application form, a power of attorney where a representative files, and location and site plans signed by an engineer registered with CREA, the Brazilian engineering council. A typical package includes:

- the application form and the applicant's company documents;
- a power of attorney for the local representative;
- location and site plans with coordinates, signed by a CREA-registered engineer, with the technical responsibility record (ART);
- a descriptive memorandum of the work, construction method and schedule;
- a hydrographic survey for dredging and works that change depths (NORMAM-501/DHN applies to surveys);
- the proposed marking during construction and any effect on existing aids to navigation;
- any other documents required by NORMAM-303 for that type of work.

Foreign contractors usually need a Brazilian legal entity or representative to file, and a CREA-registered engineer to sign the drawings. Arrange both early, because registering foreign qualifications can take time.

## Other licences may apply

A favourable opinion from the Navy deals with navigation safety and the use of the waterway. Depending on the project, you may also need:

- environmental licensing from the federal, state or municipal agency;
- authorization from the Federal Property Secretariat (SPU) to use federal land and waters;
- municipal planning and building permits;
- other federal, state and municipal licences that apply to the project and its location.

Works carried out without the Navy's opinion can be fined, embargoed or ordered to be demolished.

## Planning the timeline

- **Start with the survey.** Hydrographic data often limits both the NORMAM-303 opinion and the buoyage project.
- **Run the licences in parallel.** Do not wait for the environmental licence before preparing the Navy package, but keep the project description consistent across agencies.
- **Use the preliminary meeting** to agree on the document list and on whether an inspection will be needed.
- **Plan the temporary marking** of the work area during construction, and the permanent aids for operation.
- **Track validity.** Note the four-year validity, or the period set for dredging, and plan renewals before major phases.
